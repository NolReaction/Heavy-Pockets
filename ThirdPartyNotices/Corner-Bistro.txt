Heavy Pockets uses the Section02/S2B3 exterior architectural mesh and its associated
material textures. The paving, four table settings, chairs, menu and topiary come
from Ground and Section02/BistroProps in the same scene. The street-facing
shell was trimmed and paired with a rotated
instance to make a freestanding building. Original texture maps were resized and
adapted to Unity Standard materials. The three distance meshes were simplified,
and the far mesh samples color from the source textures at its vertices. The
prefab adds simplified collision volumes. The menu board, folding chair and
terrace table also have individual editor prefabs that share the same materials.
The original ground-floor door leaves were opened, the shop glazing was adapted
for views through the facade, and the normals of broad masonry panels were
aligned with the source facade planes. The playable dining room has a fitted
interior floor, architectural joinery and plaster mouldings. Its oak flooring
reuses the Poly Haven Wood Floor scan by Dimitrios Savva under CC0, as credited
in Architectural-floors.txt. These are modifications for the game.
