Heavy Pockets adapted these models for Unity's Standard shader, converted and
resized the material maps, simplified the meshes at two levels of detail, added
placement pivots, a spotlight and collision where appropriate, and composed the
seating and coffee-service area. The table and chair prefabs used in this room
come from the Amazon Lumberyard Bistro scene credited in Corner-Bistro.txt.
