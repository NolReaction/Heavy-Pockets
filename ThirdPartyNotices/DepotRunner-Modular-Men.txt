Depot Runner character

The character geometry is adapted from Quaternius's Ultimate Modular Men Pack
(Worker, Punk, Farmer, Swat, Casual and Casual2), February 2022.
Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html
GLB conversions: https://quaternius.trebeljahr.com/modular_men_pack
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The rest skeleton is from the Universal Base Characters Standard kit by
Quaternius, also released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html

Heavy Pockets fits the modular meshes to this skeleton, weights the toes,
separates head and hair options, sculpts a second jaw, adds eyes, lips and
garment details, and uses an original harbor workwear palette.
ArtSource/DepotRunner.blend contains the fitted
meshes and material slots. The numbered mesh variants for hair, face,
upper body, trousers and footwear share one skeleton. Matching Leg/Boot
indices form a pair at the trouser hem.
