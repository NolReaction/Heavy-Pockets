Quayside vegetation

The pine sprays and bark are derived from Pine Tree 01 by Poly Haven.
https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01
https://polyhaven.com/license

Grass and herb clumps use grassbushcc008 and grassbushcc007 from Stylized Grass
and Bush Textures by FabinhoSC. The shrub sprigs use Stylized branches Texture
by the same author.
https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-grass-and-bush-textures-0
https://opengameart.org/content/stylized-branches-texture

All three sources are CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The port uses cropped, recolored and layered foliage in a 1024 x 512 atlas
with transparent margins and coverage-preserving mipmaps. Bark is a separate
tiling 512 px texture. Distant tree silhouettes use Shore-conifers.txt.
