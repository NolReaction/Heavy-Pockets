Heavy Pockets plays its locomotion clips on a shared native character skeleton.

The public base locomotion and jump clips use Universal Animation Library
(Standard), by Quaternius. Heavy Pockets adapts directional steps, foot
contacts, turn-in-place steps and standing/travelling jump poses.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html

Slide entry, looping slide and recovery use Universal Animation Library 2
(Standard), by Quaternius.
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html

Both Quaternius libraries are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The selected sprint uses the "run" take of the three.js Xbot example,
credited to Mixamo by its contributor. Setup-Mac.command installs the run
locally. On any platform, invoke python3 Tools/Import-MixamoSprint.py
(with py in place of python3 on Windows). The importer verifies the pinned
source and retargets the performance onto the Heavy Pockets skeleton.

The native Sprint.anim stays in Assets/HeavyPockets/Resources/RunnerPrivate,
outside source control. An existing locally tuned Sprint.anim takes
precedence. Unity imports the clip on refresh and the game uses a 2.38 m
stride; the bundled Quaternius sprint is available for offline checkouts.
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/pull/18822#issuecomment-613028321
https://community.adobe.com/questions-696/mixamo-faq-licensing-royalties-ownership-eula-and-tos-589400
