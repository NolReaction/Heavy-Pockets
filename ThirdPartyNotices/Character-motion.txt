The full-body locomotion clips in Assets/HeavyPockets/Resources/FreightMotion
are adapted from Universal Animation Library (Standard), by Quaternius.

Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The clips were retargeted to the Heavy Pockets BaseBody skeleton. Retreat
and sidestep variants, contact height corrections and motion blending were
created for Heavy Pockets. The character mesh and its texture are separate
from this animation library.
