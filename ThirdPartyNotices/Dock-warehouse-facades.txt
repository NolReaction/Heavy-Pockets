Modular Factory Facade
Artist: James Ray Cock, Poly Haven
Asset: https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_factory_facade
License: CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

ReceivingWarehouse and QuayWorkshop use selected wall, window, personnel
door and rolling-shutter modules from the source model. The two shells were
laid out to fit the port's existing building footprints. Flat subdivisions
were dissolved, surfaces were combined, and the original base color, normal,
metallic and roughness textures were repacked into shared 2K atlases.
Opaque glass and paint colors were adjusted to match the port.

The receiving warehouse has a cut masonry opening, a hinged service door
and an original interior. The interior finish atlas reuses the Painted
Plaster Wall, Terrazzo Tiles and Wood Table 001 scans credited in
Chart-house-surfaces.txt under CC0. The dispatch forms and signage are
original port artwork.
